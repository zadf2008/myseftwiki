Bài 1:

1. \(\lim_{x \to 4} \frac{\sqrt{1+2x}-3}{\sqrt{x}-2}\)

\(= \lim_{x \to 4} \frac{(\sqrt{1+2x}-3)(\sqrt{1+2x}+3)(\sqrt{x}+2)}{(\sqrt{x}-2)(\sqrt{x}+2)(\sqrt{1+2x}+3)}\)

\(= \lim_{x \to 4} \frac{(2x-8)(\sqrt{x}+2)}{(x-4)(\sqrt{1+2x}+3)}\)

\(= \lim_{x \to 4} \frac{2(x-4)(\sqrt{x}+2)}{(x-4)(\sqrt{1+2x}+3)}\)

\(= \lim_{x \to 4} \frac{2(\sqrt{x}+2)}{\sqrt{1+2x}+3}\)

\(= \frac{2(\sqrt{4}+2)}{\sqrt{1+2.4}+3}\)

\(= \frac{8}{6} = \frac{4}{3}\)

2. \(\lim_{x \to 1} \frac{\sqrt[5]{x}-1}{\sqrt[4]{x}-1}\)

\(= \lim_{x \to 1} \frac{x^{\frac{1}{5}}-1}{x^{\frac{1}{4}}-1} \)

\(= \frac{\lim_{x \to 1} \frac{x^{\frac{1}{5}}-1}{x-1}}{\lim_{x \to 1} \frac{x^{\frac{1}{4}}-1}{x-1}} \)

\(= \frac{\frac{1}{5}}{\frac{1}{4}} = \frac{4}{5}\)

3. \(\lim_{x \to +\infty} (\sqrt{x + \sqrt{x + \sqrt{x}}}-\sqrt{x})\)

\(= \lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{(\sqrt{x + \sqrt{x + \sqrt{x}}}-\sqrt{x})(\sqrt{x + \sqrt{x + \sqrt{x}}}+\sqrt{x})}{(\sqrt{x + \sqrt{x + \sqrt{x}}}+\sqrt{x})}\)

\(= \lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{x + \sqrt{x + \sqrt{x}}-x}{(\sqrt{x + \sqrt{x + \sqrt{x}}}+\sqrt{x})}\)

\(= \lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{\frac{\sqrt{x + \sqrt{x}}}{\sqrt{x}}}{\frac{\sqrt{x + \sqrt{x + \sqrt{x}}}+\sqrt{x}}{\sqrt{x}}}\)

\(= \lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{\sqrt{\frac{x + \sqrt{x}}{x}}}{\sqrt{\frac{x + \sqrt{x + \sqrt{x}}}{x}}+1}\)

\(= \lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{\sqrt{1 + \frac{1}{\sqrt{x}} }}{\sqrt{1+\frac{\sqrt{x + \sqrt{x}}}{x}}+1} = \frac{\sqrt{1+0}}{\sqrt{1+0}+1} = \frac{1}{2}\)

4. \(\lim_{x \to 1} \frac{x^x-1}{x \ln x}\)

\(= \lim_{x \to 1} \frac{e^{x \ln x}-1}{x \ln x} (A^B = A^{B \ln A})\)

Đặt \(x \ln x = u(x)\)

Do \(1 \ln 1 = 0 \Rightarrow u(x) \to 0 \)

\(= \lim_{u(x) \to 0} \frac{e^{u(x)}-1}{u(x)} = 1\)

5. \(\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sqrt{1+ \sin x}-\sqrt{1- \sin x}}{x}\)

\(=\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{(\sqrt{1+ \sin x}-\sqrt{1- \sin x})(\sqrt{1+ \sin x}+\sqrt{1- \sin x})}{x(\sqrt{1+ \sin x}+\sqrt{1- \sin x})}\)

\(=\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{1+ \sin x - 1 + \sin x }{x(\sqrt{1+ \sin x}+\sqrt{1- \sin x})}\)

\(=\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{2 \ sin x}{x(\sqrt{1+ \sin x}+\sqrt{1- \sin x})}\)

\(=\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{2x}{x(\sqrt{1+\sin x}+\sqrt{1-\sin x})}\)

\(=\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{2}{\sqrt{1+\sin x}+\sqrt{1-\sin x}}\)

\(= \frac{2}{\sqrt{1+0}+\sqrt{1-0}} = \frac{2}{1+1} = 1\)

6. \(\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin (5x)}{\tan (8x)}\)

\(=\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{5x}{8x} = \frac{5}{8}\)
